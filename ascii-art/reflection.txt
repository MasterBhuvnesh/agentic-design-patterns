KEY CONCEPTS:
• Self-critique
• Iterative improvement
• Quality rubrics
• Convergence limits

SUMMARY:
Reflection generates an initial output, then
feeds it to a critic agent that evaluates it
against quality rubrics, tests, and checks.
If the output fails, structured feedback is
produced and a revision agent improves it.
This generate-critique-revise loop repeats up
to a fixed iteration limit to ensure
convergence. The pattern trades added latency
and cost for systematically higher quality by
separating creation from evaluation.
