KEY CONCEPTS:
• Dependency mapping
• Value scoring
• Dynamic reordering
• Starvation prevention

SUMMARY:
Prioritization scores queued tasks by value,
effort, urgency, and risk, then ranks them using
a formula (Priority = Value/Effort x Urgency).
Tasks are executed from the top of the ranked
queue. When a higher-priority item arrives, the
current task can be preempted and state saved.
Aging and quotas prevent low-priority starvation.
The pattern trades scoring overhead and context-
switching cost for optimal resource utilization
and responsive, fair task scheduling.
