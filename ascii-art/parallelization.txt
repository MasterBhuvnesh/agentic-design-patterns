KEY CONCEPTS:
• Independent units
• Concurrent execution
• Rate limiting
• Result aggregation

SUMMARY:
Parallelization splits a large task into
independent units and processes them concurrently
across multiple workers. A sharding step divides
the input; workers execute in parallel under a
rate limit, retrying on failure with backoff.
Once all workers finish, results are merged into
a single consolidated output. The pattern trades
coordination complexity and higher resource cost
for dramatic speed gains on workloads where
sub-tasks have no mutual dependencies.
