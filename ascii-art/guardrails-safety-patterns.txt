• Policy enforcement

SUMMARY:
Guardrails and Safety Patterns wrap processing
in multi-layer protection. Inputs are sanitized
for PII, injection attacks, and harmful content.
A risk scorer assigns a severity level that gates
further processing: low risk proceeds, medium is
limited, high requires human review, and critical
is blocked outright. Outputs pass through policy
and ethics checks before delivery. All decisions
are logged for audit. This pattern trades latency
and some false positives for robust protection
against unsafe or non-compliant behavior.
