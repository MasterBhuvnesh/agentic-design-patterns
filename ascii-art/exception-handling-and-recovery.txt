SUMMARY:
Exception Handling and Recovery classifies errors
into transient, permanent, or critical categories
and routes each to a tailored strategy. Transient
errors trigger retries with exponential backoff
and jitter. Permanent errors invoke fallbacks
such as cached data or simpler models. Critical
failures escalate to humans after saving state
via checkpoints. The system logs every failure
for pattern analysis, enabling self-improvement.
This pattern trades added complexity for graceful
degradation and resilient autonomous operation.
