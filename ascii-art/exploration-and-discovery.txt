• Pattern clustering

• Hypothesis generation

SUMMARY:
Exploration and Discovery starts with a broad
scout across diverse sources (papers, data, web,
experts), then maps and clusters findings into
themes. High-novelty or high-impact clusters are
selected for targeted deep dives. Insights from
deep investigation feed hypothesis generation,
which drives iterative experimentation looping
back into further scouting. The cycle repeats
until an iteration limit is reached, producing
a documented set of discoveries. This pattern
trades time and resources for systematic,
serendipity-enabling knowledge accumulation.
